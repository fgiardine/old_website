### About Me
I am a postdoctoral researcher in the <a href="[https://eecs.berkeley.edu](https://ece.illinois.edu/)/">Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering</a> in the Grainger College of Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. 

I completed a Ph.D. in  <a href="https://eecs.berkeley.edu/">Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences</a> 
at the University of California, Berkeley, where I was advised by Professor Robert Pilawa-Podgurski in the <a href="https://bpec.berkeley.edu/">Berkeley Power and Energy Center</a>. My dissertation focused on the design and control of high-performance multilevel power converters for single-phase DC-AC/AC-DC systems. I have a particular interest in characterizing and addressing critical challenges such as electromagnetic interference for multilevel topologies. My graduate research was generously supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.

Prior to starting my degree at Berkeley, I graduated summa cum laude with my B.S in Engineering Science and Mathematics from Smith College. Here, I was engaged in undergraduate research related to both engineering education and water resources.
